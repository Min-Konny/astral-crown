# Patrol character artwork — local candidate

Selected Leonis (sol), Ash and Fiore (flora). Leonis and Ash previously used the identical shield-knight atlas frame, including together in Garden stage 3; Fiore is the support in the same enemy team and several later stages. The six legacy collection characters remain on the atlas in this limited batch.

Existing party.png was visually inspected. Blue hair/armored knight identity is retained for both men: Leonis now has a tall ivory/gold shield and crimson cape; Ash has a two-handed sword, tied hair and narrow dark armor, with no shield. Fiore retains long pink hair, floral circlet, ivory/rose dress and staff; her open petal staff/crown and long hair distinguish her from Mira. These refine previously shared placeholders; no pre-existing individual portrait was replaced.

Generated through built-in image_gen.imagegen, transparent_background=true. No reference images, outside artwork, logos or franchise names were sent. Model/version and generation IDs were not exposed by the tool. Exact PNG data URLs were decoded into workspace assets without image edits. No external transfer service or guessed URL. Original design requests are not a non-infringement guarantee.

CHARACTER_ART in src/character-art.js is the single map for Phaser battle/cut-in and HTML collection/training/summon results. Legacy frame numbers remain the failure fallback; Luna's full portrait is unchanged. No gameplay, save, rewards or capability changes.
