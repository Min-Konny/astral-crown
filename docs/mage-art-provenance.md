# Mage group artwork — local candidate

Selected リュネ (luna), 若葉の術師 (sage), 見習い魔術師 (scholar): all three still shared frame 0 of party.png, the largest remaining identical-sprite group. Existing luna.png and party.png were visually inspected. Luna retains silver-lavender long hair, crescent ornament, blue eyes, navy/ivory robes and an armillary scepter. The two legacy mages retain pale hair and robes but gain book/short-hair and hat/wand/braid silhouettes respectively. They remain legacy-only characters; no combat eligibility or abilities were added.

Built-in image_gen.imagegen, transparent_background=true, three separate calls. No reference image, external art, logo or franchise name sent. Model/version and generation IDs were not exposed. Returned PNG dataURL bytes were decoded unchanged into assets; no image edits or guessed transfer URLs. This records original design requests, not a guarantee of rights clearance.

The shared CHARACTER_ART map covers battle, collection, training, result portraits and generic summon cut-ins. Luna's existing home and large summon portrait still use luna.png intentionally; her new chibi is used on cards and in battle. All three retain frame 0 fallback.
